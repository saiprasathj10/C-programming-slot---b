1\. **Cab Fare Calculation**



A cab service calculates the final fare based on the distance travelled and the customer's membership. The base fare is ₹100. If the distance travelled is more than 10 km, an additional ₹20 is charged for every kilometer beyond 10 km. Customers with a membership receive a 10% discount on the final fare. Develop a C program to calculate the amount payable.



**Sample Input**

Distance: 15

Member: true

**Sample Output**

Final Fare: ₹180

**SAMPLE INPUT:**

100

1

10

**SAMPLE OUTPUT:**

110.00

0.20

109.80
